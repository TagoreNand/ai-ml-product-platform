# Event Taxonomy

## Core product events
- workspace_created
- onboarding_step_completed
- workflow_published
- workflow_run_succeeded
- workflow_run_failed
- feature_viewed
- feature_activated
- dashboard_shared
- api_token_created
- support_ticket_created

## Why it matters
Good AI/ML products depend on consistent event semantics. PM, analytics, and MLE teams must agree on what counts as activation, healthy usage, and intervention triggers.
